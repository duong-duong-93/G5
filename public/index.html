Hidden Five

Five tiles sit on your stand and everyone can read them except you. Ask for clues, cross out what cannot be yours, and be first to name all five.

How a turn goes

  1. Reveal. Draw a tile of any colour from the supply. Its number is now public.
  2. Ask. Pick one tile from the pool. Sort tells you where it fits among your five (a tile on your stand is always in ascending order). Compare tells you whether it has the same number of dots as one slot of your choice.
  3. Call it. At any moment you may name your five numbers. One try only. A wrong call puts you out; the last player standing wins.

60 tiles, numbers 1 to 60, five colours of twelve, each with 1, 2 or 3 dots. Everyone starts with one tile of each colour.
